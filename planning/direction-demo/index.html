01 / DELEGATE

Agree once. Act within bounds.

Confirm goals, values, philosophy, and measures. Approved mandates let trusted signals start scoped work without a new prompt. Inherit limits, budgets, and owners; reuse existing tools.

02 / CORRECT

Close the loop before handoff.

Native checks and runtime probes feed actionable failures to the worker. Repair, recheck, and explain which standards apply. Missing evidence or authority means blocked; judgment is never disguised as a deterministic pass.

03 / OWN

Passing checks is not the verdict.

Follow results against the goal, not task counts. Personal learning becomes team policy only through review and activation. Keep current instructions lean and every key decision traceable from day one.

              What makes the loop trustworthy · proposed operating contract
            
            
    One task, bounded work
    
            
    
              Reuse the existing request or tracker for objective, non-goals,
              permitted operations, and acceptance. No new policy record for
              every edit. Check declared limits mechanically; use skill judgment
              for task relevance and taste. Preserve unrelated work. Repeated
              failures, oscillating repairs, or the agreed retry/time budget
              produce a precise blocker. Missing authority stops immediately.
            
    
            
    Approved mandates can initiate work
    
            
    
              A mission explains why; a mandate grants permission. Match a
              fresh, trusted observation to an active owner-approved mandate
              with scope, allowed actions, expiry, budgets, and stop conditions.
              Reproduce heuristic findings before repair. Existing schedulers
              invoke checks; the existing tracker and agent runtime own task
              state and execution. Deduplicate events, claim one worker, enforce
              cooldowns and limits, and recheck revocation before side effects.
              Never start work from an inferred goal alone. Merge, release, and
              sharing remain separate permissions; wh check itself never starts
              a repair or publishes.
            
    
            
    Standing authority, not standing permission to do anything
    
            
    
              Inherit owner-approved defaults for permitted actions, excluded
              resources, retry/time/cost limits, and suspension triggers. Match
              each task to that grant; do not infer it from green checks. Map
              restrictions to host permissions and protected platform gates.
              Unsupported constraints are advisory, never advertised as
              enforced. Standing code-work authority never authorizes wh push:
              sharing still requires explicit selection and confirmation.
            
    
            
    Final verification is more than green output
    
            
    
              Verify the final task diff, acceptance conditions, applicable
              native checks, and required reviews. Check failures return
              location, rationale, repair direction, and the next verification
              action. The worker repairs; the CLI rechecks. Unknown, stale,
              skipped required work, and checker errors cannot satisfy
              completion. Broader completion checks catch changes missed by
              per-file hooks; protected CI remains independent.
            
    
            
    Verification, authorization, and outcome are separate
    
            
    
              wh check evaluates the exact candidate. An accountable owner can
              accept, redirect, or reject it for product fit, cost, or
              operational risk—even when checks pass. Record consequential
              verdicts through wh dash or wh change, linked to the existing
              request or PR. Routine actions may use an explicit standing grant
              within real gates; merge, release, migration, and policy
              publication retain their own authority boundaries. A second
              agent’s review is advice, not an independent guarantee or a
              substitute for the responsible owner.
            
    
            
    
              Accepted, required, installed, and experimental are distinct
            
    
            
    
              A protected activation record selects the required policy and
              check/configuration digests for a code scope or environment.
              Acceptance does not automatically activate it. Receipts identify
              the exact code snapshot, policy, checker, scope, and evidence age.
              Evaluate mandatory team checks unchanged; experiments have
              separate results, not override precedence. A local conflict cannot
              downgrade team requirements. Offline checks identify their cached
              snapshot and cannot claim remote freshness.
            
    
            
    Importing instructions is not permission to execute them
    
            
    
              Preview each new or modified executable, configuration, working
              directory, outputs, and required access. Untrusted bindings return
              needs_execution_approval without executing. Reuse isolated native
              runners with restricted credentials. Agent context and private
              preferences cannot override authorization. Hosted inspection
              should be read-only by default; editing and execution require
              explicit authentication and capability checks.
            
    
            
    A decision-ready, resumable handoff
    
            
    
              Keep a stable task/change ID, current revision, required policy,
              blocking questions, permitted next actions, and expected effects.
              Replies carry the expected revision; stale answers are rejected
              and repeated requests do not duplicate changes. Approval binds the
              exact reviewed content. Changing that content requires renewed
              review. Group repeated requests for the same decision. Automatic
              correction never silently teaches a new team rule.
            
    
            
    Selected sharing, complete references
    
            
    
              wh push previews a package of selected changes and required
              dependencies missing from the shared base. A private or
              unavailable dependency blocks publication until a deliberately
              shareable replacement exists. Confirmation binds package digest,
              destination, and base revision. Changed or merged proposals return
              to review. Private records and ancestry remain outside the
              replicated path; a plain database push is not the sharing
              contract.
            
    
            
    Personal learning is not team authority
    
            
    
              Facts, suggestions, preferences, rules, and permissions have
              different roles. Capture meeting notes and memories only as
              explicitly selected candidate sources. Compatible local
              preferences may narrow choices; conflicts cannot override
              mandatory team rules. Team mode requires an authenticated
              authorized human reviewer other than the proposer; agents inherit
              their principal's identity. Solo mode allows explicit
              self-approval. Preview a deliberately shareable proposal, not
              private memory or its ancestry. Content or base changes require
              renewed review; approval never activates wiring.
            
    
            
    Explain applicability and deliver one governed context
    
            
    
              Match standards to task intent, components, operations, and
              environment as well as paths. Show the reason, goal, source,
              relevant evidence, uncertainty, and policy revision. Agent
              relevance judgment cannot remove mandatory checks. Business goals
              inform judgment, not arbitrary file-level failures; passing tests
              cannot prove test-first development without process evidence.
              Generate scoped projections from one approved source, including
              rule IDs, policy/checker digests, adapter revision, and delivery
              freshness. Unsupported host capabilities stay visible. Consistent
              source material does not guarantee identical behavior across
              models.
            
    
            
    Measures guide decisions; safeguards have a stage
    
            
    
              Link a mission outcome to its source, owner, and revisit trigger.
              For example, successful checkout journeys are an outcome; latency
              and failure rate are safeguards. Label checks as local, merge,
              release, or observational. Production health cannot prove a new
              revision works. A necessary reliability/security fix can use an
              owner-approved scoped exception with expiry and follow-up; its
              missing health evidence remains unknown.
            
    
            
    
              Pair near-term signals with longer-term outcomes and
              countervailing safeguards. Compare compatible cohorts and
              definitions; before/after movement alone does not prove causality.
              No effect is a useful result, not a reason to invent success.
              Owners approve metric revisions with rationale; keep old
              definitions and observations inspectable.
            
    
            
    Acceptance includes follow-through
    
            
    
              For consequential changes, record expected effect, observation
              source, cohort, freshness, owner, review window, and recovery
              path. Link existing telemetry and incident/release tools. A breach
              opens a scoped investigation and actionable agent feedback;
              missing or stale data stays unknown and reaches the observation
              owner. Local repair cannot close a live incident. Rollback and
              production writes need explicit authority; a newly released
              revision needs its own observation. Repeated failures can propose
              tests or guidance via wh change, never silently modify accepted
              rules.
            
    
            
    Test the loop, and preserve the reasoning
    
            
    
              Keep a small regression set: a real violation gets repaired; a
              valid alternative is not falsely blocked; a stale approval or
              changed checker cannot pass; narrow tests do not hide a failed
              user requirement; missing authority escalates to the right owner.
              Rerun when models, skills, or integrations change. Store accepted
              and rejected preference examples with rationale. Stable links join
              intent → constraint → change → verification → decision → outcome;
              send agents only the relevant slice, not the full history. Dolt
              records and portable interfaces suffice—no graph database or
              dependency on one agent’s internals.
            
    
            
    Start small and prove useful correction
    
            
    
              First slice: one repository, one existing CI/release integration,
              local Dolt, this dashboard, full recorded decision history, and
              optional selective sharing. Adopt a few checks with known-bad and
              known-good cases; existing debt is explicit, never silently
              forgiven. Measure time to a trusted safeguard, mistakes corrected
              before handoff, false blocks, correction time, unnecessary owner
              interruptions, escaped failures, decision effort, and overdue
              follow-through. Zero interruptions alone is not success. Defer
              custom telemetry, agent fleet management, broad adapter coverage,
              and organization-wide administration. Keep relationships as stable
              links; a graph editor is not required.
            
    
            
    Keep context current without losing history
    
            
    
              Completion events can prompt reference audits and archive
              proposals. Age or similarity alone never authorizes deletion.
              Preserve original sources, hashes, rationale, alternatives, and
              supersession links; prove retrieval and reference integrity before
              excluding reviewed obsolete material from active context. Changed
              content invalidates approval. Current retrieval omits superseded
              advice; historical as-of queries retain it. Review unresolved
              overlap instead of silently merging requirements. Recovery is a
              new recorded change.
            
    
            
    A component, not a replacement workspace
    
            
    
              Whetstone governs engineering agreements and enforcement feedback.
              Beads can track work and Virgil can provide conversation and
              coordination; neither is required for local use. Dolt remains the
              proposed record store, not the approval mechanism. Evaluate reuse
              through an integration boundary before assuming Beads memories
              supply governance or durable policy storage. Keep the six
              commands; people see goals, work, decisions, and results before
              terminal output.
            
    
            
    
              Direction informed by
              Addy Osmani’s Own the Outer Loop: automate routine correction while keeping consequential
              decisions and follow-through accountable. This is a proposed
              Whetstone design, not an implemented control plane.
